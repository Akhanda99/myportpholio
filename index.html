Hi! I'm Muntasir Mubin Nashit

I'm doing my MSc in Computer Science at the University of Manitoba, where I research Human-Robot Interaction in the HCI Lab under Dr. Houda Elmimouni. Before this, I completed my undergraduate degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Khulna University of Engineering and Technology (KUET).

My work focuses on teleoperation for telepresence robots, designing control systems that make them easier and more natural for first-time users to operate. More broadly, I'm interested in social robots and the social-psychological side of human-robot interaction, from how people control robots to how identity, comfort, and inclusion shape the way we interact through them.

Research Projects

Research Project Human-Robot Interaction 2025

Group Dynamics in Human-Robot Collaboration

An exploratory study of how a remote person, joining through a telepresence robot, is included when collaborating with a co-located group. It examines attention, leadership, and communication as team size changes.
Collaboration with the University of Southampton.

Telepresence·Mixed Methods·Video Coding·Thematic Analysis

Research Project Human-Robot Interaction Ongoing 2026

Identity Presentation & Social Exposure in Telepresence

A study on how the way a remote operator presents themselves through a telepresence robot shapes their comfort and social behavior when interacting with people in public.
Collaboration with the University of Salerno.

Telepresence·Experiment Design·Mixed Methods·User Study

Graduate Course Project Human-Robot Interaction 2026

TiltControl: Tilt-Based Teleoperation of the e-puck
